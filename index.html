THE VISION

Turn everyday work into an experience.

Instead of ordinary background music, imagine choosing video content you enjoy. BEAST AI could adapt the experience to your work context, interests, skills and progress — while turning real tasks into optional, personalized challenges.

What BEAST AI could bring together

01 · Video Radio

A personalized stream of suitable video scenes, audio and content based on your preferences.

02 · Work Context

AI that can understand tasks and workflow with the user's permission, then adapt the experience.

03 · Personal Challenges

Turn goals and completed tasks into levels, milestones and optional game-like challenges.

04 · Adaptive Experience

Adjust the experience using user feedback and, optionally, consent-based smartwatch activity data.

A SIMPLE EXAMPLE

A developer's workday, reimagined.

You're working through a challenging task. BEAST AI could help make the experience more engaging with a fitting video atmosphere and a small optional challenge matched to your goals and progress — without getting in the way of concentration.
